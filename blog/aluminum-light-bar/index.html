Aluminum Fabrication

Aluminum Light Bar

This made-to-order aluminum light bar with mud flap hangers was built around the truck, the light layout, and the customer’s dimensions instead of a universal off-the-shelf pattern.

After Dark KreationsJun 25, 20222 min read
All shop notesMounts + bracketsRequest a light bar

Built around the truck

The original project was a Peterbilt 359 layout with seven watermelon lights across the center and four 4-inch round lights on each side over the mud flap hangers. ADK builds these to fit the truck specifically, so the customer’s dimensions and desired light layout matter.

Frame mounts

The frame mounts are made from 1/4-inch thick aluminum and built from customer-provided dimensions so they follow the frame profile. The mount style requires drilling four holes through the frame, which helps reduce vibration, creates a solid attachment point, and supports long-term durability. The brackets are TIG welded to the light bar all the way around.

Light bar construction

The light bar uses 6-inch by 3-inch by .188 aluminum tube with the tube ends capped in 1/4-inch aluminum. Watermelon lights use 1 3/16-inch wiring holes and tapped mounting points. The 4-inch lights use 4.5-inch holes for rubber gasket mounting or safety rings. Additional side or rear lighting can be planned depending on the design.

Mud flap hangers and finish

Mud flap hangers are made from 2-inch by 2-inch by .25-inch aluminum angle and stitch welded in place. The hanger offset lets the mud flap sit flush with the front edge when installed. ADK can also pair the setup with custom mirrored stainless ADK weights.

Lead time and shipping

The original lead time was 3 to 4 weeks. Parts came ready for paint with a DA finish. Polishing can be discussed, but lights and hardware are not included unless specified. Because these bars are dimensionally large, shipping can be higher than expected, so a shipping quote is worth confirming before ordering.
