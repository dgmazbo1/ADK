Custom Truck Parts

Manual Train Horn Valve Cover

The ADK manual train horn valve cover gives a truck an old-school controlled horn feel while hiding the bulky manual valve in a cleaner, custom-fabricated housing.

After Dark KreationsJun 29, 20222 min read
All shop notesShop fabricated partsRequest a part

What it does

An electric train horn valve is basically on or off. A manual valve lets the driver control airflow and shape the sound, which gives the horn a more authentic old-school feel. The ADK cover cleans up the install, hides the bulky valve, and can be finished to match the truck interior.

Valve fitment

The original ADK part was designed around HornBlasters valve setups or the Graham White 353 1/2-inch manual valve. Customers have finished these covers polished, painted, powder coated, color matched, and even leather wrapped to make the interior detail fit the truck.

Install planning

Placement matters. The cover should sit on the door side of the driver seat where it is comfortable to reach but not easy to bump. Before drilling, verify the underside of the cab has clearance for mounting hardware and air lines. The bracket can be used as a drill template, with pre-drilled holes for 3/8-inch bolts.

Airline and hardware notes

Use the provided 1/2-inch NPT swivel to 1/2-inch DOT PTC fittings and snug them carefully to help prevent leaks. Cut DOT airline square, remove scratched or nicked ends, and seat the line fully in the PTC fittings. ADK recommends plumbing the valve first, then finishing the remaining routing after the part is mounted.

Fitment standard

Each unit is test fit before it leaves ADK. If there is an issue or concern, the point is to solve it, not leave the customer guessing. That is the same standard ADK brings to mounts, brackets, air ride components, and one-off truck parts.
