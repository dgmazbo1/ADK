Steer Axle Air Ride Management Options

Steer axle air ride can make a big rig more comfortable and more controlled, but the management setup has to match how the truck is driven, loaded, and adjusted.

After Dark KreationsSep 27, 20243 min read
All shop notesShop ADKAsk about air ride

The basics

Steer axle air ride management uses air pressure and control hardware to absorb road shock, tune ride height, and help the driver manage comfort and control. ADK has offered three practical variations depending on how much control the driver wants.

Option 1: dual leveling valves

The dual leveling valve setup uses two Hendrickson-style leveling valves with rods and a factory Paccar dump switch. Air is supplied to the switch with 1/4-inch DOT airline, then routed from the switch to both leveling valves. The system can dump the suspension or return to a preset ride height after the linkage rods are trimmed and set.

Option 2: four-valve manifold with Viair dual needle gauge

This setup gives the driver more direct control of ride height on the fly. The original ADK article notes a Slam Specialties two-corner 3/8-inch PTC manifold with 1/8-inch NPT gauge ports rated to 200 PSI, particulate trap, 30 feet of 3/8-inch DOT airline, 30 feet of 1/4-inch DOT airline, two momentary toggle switches, fittings, and a Viair 2-inch black illuminated dual needle pressure gauge rated to 160 PSI.

Option 3: four-valve manifold with Tel Tek gauge

The Tel Tek version uses the same style of four-valve manifold control but pairs it with a dual-display load pressure gauge. The Tel Tek gauge is made for a standard 2 1/16-inch hole and gives a direct pressure readout for monitoring tractor and trailer air bag pressure.

Maintenance and troubleshooting

Inspect for air leaks, worn components, debris in valves, airline damage, and alignment issues. Uneven tire wear can point to imbalance or alignment problems. A rough ride can mean low pressure or worn components. Excessive bounce can indicate overinflated air springs or a setup that needs adjustment.

For air ride fabrication, suspension modification, or fitment review, send ADK photos of the truck, existing hardware, routing constraints, and the ride-height goals.
